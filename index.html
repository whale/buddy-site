Buddy is a micro-do-list for distraction-prone super-noticers. It's the cutest, easiest-to-use, constrained task list in history.

To hell with big list energy. Six things is the limit because size matters.

Go on — add something. Anything. That thing you shouldn't forget to do.

Try it. Add a 5th thing and Buddy blushes. See what happens when you add a 6th! That's it. Can't add more. Limits are our friends.

Hover a task and check it off. Party time. Everything you finish stacks up in today's done list. Feels good to spy on what you have done!

Tucked out of the way on the right of your screen until you forget what you were doing. Then just tap the right side and — whammo — relief in reminder form. You're welcome.
